Ten checks

Results appear here after a verification runs.

How scoring works

Each check carries a weight. Site reachability and contract checks weigh most at 15 points each. Team, token, costs, marketing, domain age, and social weigh 10 each. Faucet and payout weigh 5 each.

A fail on any check means SKIP. An unknown result on the site, contract, or token check means CAUTION. A score below 70 means CAUTION. Everything else is GRIND.

Honest limits

Sites behind bot protection cannot be read, and that check reports unknown instead of guessing. Pages that render content with JavaScript may show fewer links than a browser sees, because GrindGuard reads the static HTML. No code can prove what a team intends to do. A verdict is a starting point for your own check, not a promise. Verdicts stay in this session and are never published.

AI synthesis runs only when a Groq API key is configured on the server. Without a key, the verdict is purely rule based and the page says so. When synthesis is on, the check results and the program URL are sent to Groq.
